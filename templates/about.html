About ConTech

Revolutionizing Concrete Monitoring with IoT and AI

Project Overview

ConTech is a cutting-edge semester project developed by students at GLA University, focusing on an innovative concrete curing monitoring system that transforms traditional construction practices.

The project combines IoT technology and real-time sensor data to monitor curing conditions, ensuring optimal strength development and structural safety.

Wireless sensors, data analytics, and cloud access together empower professionals to make informed, proactive decisions for better construction outcomes.

The Problem

Challenges in traditional concrete curing:

  • No real-time data during critical curing phases
  • Use of destructive testing methods
  • Unoptimized strength development
  • Project delays due to manual inspections
  • Inconsistent quality control

Our Solution

ConTech solves this with:

  • Embedded wireless sensors for key metrics
  • Cloud-based real-time monitoring
  • Non-destructive, continuous data tracking
  • Proactive alerts & automation
  • Remote access via dashboard

Technical Architecture

Our Sensor Modules

At ConTech, we use a range of smart sensors to track real-time moisture and temperature during concrete curing, ensuring durability and optimal strength development.

ESP8266 CH340 NodeMCU

Enables real-time data communication and connectivity.

  • Built-in Wi-Fi module
  • Microcontroller unit (MCU)
  • Seamless data transmission to the cloud

Moisture Sensor Module

Optimizes moisture levels throughout curing.

  • Measures moisture content in concrete
  • Analog & digital output
  • Reduces under/over-curing risks

DHT22 Temperature & Humidity Sensor

Ensures proper environmental monitoring during curing.

  • High-precision readings
  • Temperature: -40 to +80°C
  • Humidity: 0–100%

DS18B20 Waterproof Temperature Sensor

Offers durable and precise temperature tracking in wet conditions.

  • Waterproof & rugged design
  • ±0.5°C accuracy
  • Ideal for long-distance and water-based setups

Why Sensor-Based Monitoring?

Replacing outdated curing methods, sensor-based monitoring offers:

  • Real-time environmental tracking
  • Smart alerts for anomalies
  • Data-backed decision-making
  • Improved strength, quality, and reliability
