Our Smart Concrete Sensors

ConTech uses Wireless Concrete Sensors to monitor Temperature, Strength, and Humidity in real-time, ensuring Faster, Safer, and more Economical construction.

Real-Time Monitoring

Track Temperature, Humidity, and Strength 24/7 with high precision.

Our embedded sensors stream real-time data continuously to the cloud, enabling site engineers to make informed decisions instantly. This reduces manual testing and ensures consistency throughout the curing process.

Data Analytics

Analyze curing trends and get AI-driven insights for optimal strength.

Historical and live data is processed using machine learning models, offering early warnings for abnormal curing patterns. Our dashboard visualizes this data in easy-to-read charts, making complex decisions simple.

Wireless & Easy

No wires, no hassle! Sensors are embedded directly in the concrete.

Installation is plug-and-play. Just place the sensor during the pour — no extra drilling or wiring required. Data transmission is handled over low-power protocols like Wi-Fi or LoRaWAN for efficiency.

Why Choose Contech?

Traditional curing methods often lead to inefficiencies and structural weaknesses. ConTech’s smart monitoring system brings precision and reliability to the process.

Enhanced Durability

Optimize curing conditions to achieve maximum concrete strength and longevity.

Proper curing ensures reduced microcracking, better load-bearing capacity, and longer structural lifespan, significantly decreasing the need for maintenance.

Cost-Efficiency

Save on construction costs by reducing material wastage and preventing rework.

By knowing exactly when the concrete has reached the required strength, you can strip formwork early and reduce project downtime, saving both time and money.

Eco-Friendly Approach

Smart monitoring minimizes excessive resource usage, lowering carbon footprint.

Efficient curing reduces unnecessary water use and overdesign, promoting sustainable practices and meeting green construction standards.

How ConTech Works ?

Our system integrates IoT sensors, AI-powered analytics, and a cloud-based dashboard to deliver real-time insights.

1. Sensor Deployment

Sensors are embedded in fresh concrete to begin monitoring.

2. Data Collection

Temperature and strength readings are uploaded to a Cloud database.

3. AI-Based Analysis

Machine learning models predict optimal curing conditions.

4. Alerts & Reports

Receive real-time alerts and generate reports for Quality-assurance.
